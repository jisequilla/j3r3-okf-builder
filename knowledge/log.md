## 2026-11-04
* **Update**: okf-review gains an audit mode (okf-flz.5): on "audit the bundle", "knowledge audit" or "what is missing", optionally since a commit, tag or date the person names, it groups `okf_lint --json`'s findings and metrics by what a person must do (rotate a secret, restore a fragment's material, verify again, distill, cite, fix a link, fill a section, answer or drop a question); the new script `okf_audit.py` (read-only git: `log`, `diff`, `rev-parse`, `show`, `ls-files`, `hash-object` without `-w`) reports the concepts deleted or renamed since the reference point, with lint's exclusions and a rename not yet added to git found by identical content, that no deprecation at the reference point, no **Deprecation** entry (or one saying deprecated, removed or superseded) added since to the root or the directory's `log.md`, and for a rename no entry naming both paths records, the audit's own entries never counting (`DELETED_UNRECORDED`, `RENAMED_UNRECORDED`), skipped with a notice without a reference point or outside git, and reads the findings earlier audits at the same reference point accepted as is; the agent proposes the findings that need judgment (unwritten terms and systems, missing sections, unfootnoted claims, `SOURCE_UNCITED`, contradictions outside the Conflict Log, `LINK_TO_RETIRED`, free-text records a ledger does not cover), never editing an ADR's body except to remove and rotate a committed secret; the person decides each finding and one **Update** entry records the audit. Standard §6 (both copies) gains the row "Audit the bundle"; the knowledge-architect agent routes an audit to it; `make test` runs the script's tests and the M365 okf-review ships it.
* **Update**: okf-distill keeps a coverage ledger (okf-flz.4): the `# Distillation` table has one row per part of `# Record`, its Concept a link, `tracker` or `no concept (<reason>)`, shown with the change set before approval; lint reports a distilled fragment without one (`FRAGMENT_NO_LEDGER`) and a row or record heading left unaccounted for (`LEDGER_GAP`), and a complete ledger counts as covered for `FRAGMENT_UNCITED`. Standard §4 and §7 (both copies) and the okf-distill, okf-review and okf-lint skills describe them; okf-review's verification fragment and released fragments write the Concept cell as the link to the concept. The last row of the [KDD bundle review](/fragments/2026-10-31-kdd-bundle-review.md) ('KDD knowledge in its own monorepo') has Concept `tracker`, decided by human:jisequilla on 2026-11-04; the pending fragments are unchanged.
* **Creation**: [ADR-0029](/base/decisions/ADR-0029-fragment-material-and-repository.md): a Knowledge Fragment may record its frozen `material` (`{ path, sha256 }`) and a `repository` at a full commit, declared with a new one-level `map` field type; lint hashes material found in the bundle and a changed file is the error `FRAGMENT_MATERIAL_CHANGED`, while `BAD_MATERIAL` and `BAD_REPOSITORY` are warnings; okf-fragment adds the meeting discipline. From decision 4 of the [migration-tool review](/fragments/2026-11-02-migration-tool-okf-review.md); decided by human:jisequilla in answers to structured questions on 2026-11-03 (a short ADR) and 2026-11-04 (the `map` type, the error level); supersedes nothing (okf-flz.3).
* **Update**: standard §4, §4.1 and §7 (both copies), the built-in Domain Profile, the fragment template and the okf-fragment and okf-lint skills describe `material`, `repository`, the `map` field type and the three new codes (okf-flz.3).
