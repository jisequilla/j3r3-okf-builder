---
name: okf-review
description: Runs the double check of an OKF bundle. Lists what changed since a reference point, the changes to its Domain Profile, the concepts that break it, the open and proposed conflicts of the Conflict Log, the open questions by owner and the draft concepts no person has verified, then records the person's decision on each conflict. Its audit mode groups lint's findings by what a person must do, adds the concepts deleted or renamed unrecorded since a reference point, the concepts edited after their instance closed and the gaps that need judgment, and records the person's decision on each finding. Use when the user says "double check", "what is pending review", "review the knowledge changes", "resolve this conflict", "audit the bundle", "knowledge audit", "what is missing", "okf review", or after okf-distill has left a fragment in conflict.
---

# okf-review

> **Paths.** `<skills>` is the directory that holds the `okf-*` skills (the parent of this skill): `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills` in the Claude plugin; `.claude/skills`, `.agents/skills`, `.github/skills` or `.gemini/skills` in a repo. The scripts live in `<skills>/okf-standard/scripts/` and the templates in `<skills>/okf-standard/templates/`. Without uv, `python3` works if PyYAML is installed.

The double check: shows a person what changed in the bundle, what is in dispute and what nobody has reviewed, and records what that person decides. It decides nothing itself.

When the person asks for an audit ("audit the bundle", "knowledge audit", "what is missing", optionally since a commit, tag or date), follow **Audit mode** below instead of the steps.

## Steps

1. **Find the bundle** (`knowledge/index.md`, searching upwards from the cwd; if there is none, propose `okf-init` and stop), **its version** (`okf_version` in the root `index.md`; if missing, `okf_lint.py --json` → `okf_version`) and **its OKF profile** (`okf_profile` in the root `index.md`; if missing, knowledge/2.0, the older OKF profile). Write in that version and in that OKF profile's layout.
   - **knowledge/2.0 bundle**: the typed directories sit at the root; drop the `base/` prefix from every directory, bundle path and `--dir` below (`/decisions/x.md`; indexes: `--dir decisions`, and `--dir fragments` when a fragment changed), never mix the two layouts, and offer the migration to knowledge/3.0 with the `okf-migrate` skill, applied only after the person confirms.
   - **The Domain Profile in force** (knowledge/3.0; a knowledge/2.0 bundle has none): read it with `uv run "<skills>/okf-standard/scripts/okf_lint.py" <bundle> --profiles <dir> --json`, not from `base/profile.md` by hand, and resolve its aliases when reading concepts (a `Fragmento` with `ingerido: false` is a pending `Knowledge Fragment` when the aliases say so).
2. **Pick the reference point**: the one the person gives (a commit, tag, branch or date); by default, the date of the last review recorded in `knowledge/log.md` (the latest `**Verification**` entry or `**Conflict**` entry that resolves or dismisses a conflict), or the most recent date section if there is none. Say which one is used, so the person can widen it.
3. **List the changes since then.** If the bundle is in Git (`git -C <bundle> rev-parse --is-inside-work-tree`), take them from Git: `git diff --name-status <ref> -- <bundle>` for a ref, or `git log --since=<date> --name-status --format='%h %ad %s' -- <bundle>` for a date, plus `git status --porcelain -- <bundle>` for what is not committed. Otherwise take them from the `log.md` entries of those dates. One line per concept: path, change, log label.
   - **Domain Profile changes**, as their own section: `git diff <ref> -- <bundle>/base/profile.md` (for a date, the `git log` above restricted to that file), plus every `extends` entry added, removed or pinned to another commit (`<path>@<commit>`), each listed with the layer it changes (`base/profile.md`, or the `extends` reference) and what it adds or changes (a type, a field, a family, a rule, an alias, a question). Without Git, the log entries on `base/profile.md`.
4. **List the conflicts** with state `open` or `proposed` in the Conflict Log (`/conflict-log.md` unless the bundle places it elsewhere; no Conflict Log means no conflicts): ID, source fragment, description, proposed resolution.
5. **List the concepts awaiting human verification**: take the drafts (`grep -rl "^status: draft" <bundle>/base <bundle>/*.md`; in 2.0, `grep -rl "^status: draft" <bundle>`), leave out the `Knowledge Fragment` and `Conflict Log` concepts, aliases resolved and local fragment types included (they stay drafts and are never verified) and keep those whose frontmatter has no `verified` entry with a `by` starting with `human:` (a `human:` in `generated` does not count). Show path, `type` and `generated`.
6. **List the concepts that break the Domain Profile** (knowledge/3.0): `uv run "<skills>/okf-standard/scripts/okf_lint.py" <bundle> --json`, the findings with a `PROFILE_*` code or a code a layer declares (the built-in Domain Profile's codes are listed in the `okf-lint` skill), one line per concept with the code, the message and the `source`, the layer that declared the broken item. A finding is fixed in the concept, or raised to the person as a Domain Profile change (`okf-capture`); never by relaxing the Domain Profile.
7. **List the open questions**: the `Open Question` concepts, aliases resolved (`grep -rl "^type: Open Question" <bundle>/base`, plus each local name of it; in 2.0, `grep -rl "^type: Open Question" <bundle>`), grouped by `owner`. For each one with `question_status: open`: path, title, `blocks`, age and `review_on`. The age counts from the `occurred_at` of its `asked_in` fragment; without one, from its `generated.at`. Mark **due** when `review_on` is before today, as `okf-lint` does (`QUESTION_REVIEW_DUE`). Flag every `question_status: answered` without `answered_by`, and every question without `owner`. Answered and dropped questions are not listed otherwise.
8. **Present the lists** and ask the person what to do with each conflict, one at a time:
   - **Resolved**, because the knowledge changed: a resolution that changes a decision is a new decision of the same type that supersedes the old one, created with `okf-capture`; anything else is an **Update** of the concept, or a deprecation linking its replacement, applied with the `okf-capture` rules (concepts under `base/<dir>/`, bundle paths `/base/...`; never a concept in `projections/` or at the bundle root). Every concept the resolution creates, updates or deprecates, a new decision included, cites the source fragment in `sources` (`resource: /fragments/<file>`) with `[^id]` footnotes on the claims it supports.
   - **Dismissed**, because it was a misunderstanding: the reason is required.
   - Not decided yet: the row stays `open`, or `proposed` with the resolution suggested.
   Suggest a resolution if useful, but never pick one: only the person decides.
   For an open question the person says no longer needs an answer: `question_status: dropped`, the reason under `# Answer`, a new `generated.at` and an **Update** log entry; its `status` does not change and it is never deleted. A question the person answers is answered with the `okf-capture` rules (the answering concept cites the question; the question gets `answered_by`).
9. **Record each decision** in the conflict's row: resolution, state (`resolved` / `dismissed` / `proposed`) and who decided, as the person's `human:<id>` (ask for it). Add `* **Conflict**: C-NNN resolved|dismissed by human:<id>: <resolution>` to `knowledge/log.md` under today's date, plus the **Creation** / **Update** / **Deprecation** entries of what the resolution changed.
10. **Release the fragment**: when none of a fragment's conflicts is `open` or `proposed` any more, set its `distillation_status: distilled` and update that item's row in its `# Distillation` ledger: its Outcome, and its Concept cell the link to the concept the resolution went into (or `no concept (<reason>)` when a dismissal changed none). Nothing else in the fragment changes.
11. **Recorded verification** (ADR-0033). Offer to verify the concepts the person has reviewed, and write `verified` only from that person's recorded answer, given in one of two channels:
    - **A structured question**: ask with the platform's question tool (the person picks an option or types a reply), never from a chat reply. Name each concept by bundle path, with the repository commit (`git rev-parse --short HEAD`) and its `generated.at`.
    - **A review page private to the person**: a page only that person's account can answer on (such as a Claude artifact shared with nobody) that shows each concept's text with its bundle path, the commit and its `generated.at`, and stores per concept the choice, the version shown and the time of the answer. Read the stored answers only when the person asks you to in the session, and apply none whose `generated_at` is not the concept's current `generated.at`: ask again for that concept.
    - If neither channel is available, do not write `verified`: hand the person the list to verify by hand.
    - Only the person named in `human:<id>` answers, for themselves. A concept they do not confirm stays `draft`.
    - Keep the question and the answer, or the stored answers as the page holds them with the page's version, verbatim as a fragment with `okf-fragment`: `origin` this agent session or the page, `participants` the person and you, `occurred_at` the time of the answer, `distillation_status: distilled`, and in `# Distillation` one row per concept (Kind `—`, Outcome `verified` or `not confirmed`, Concept the link to that concept, `/base/...`).
    - Then, for each confirmed concept, add `verified: { by: human:<id>, at: <time of the answer> }` and `status: stable`, and log one `* **Verification**: ...` entry naming the concepts and linking the fragment.
    - A concept edited after the question was asked or the page was built (its `generated.at` changed) is asked again. Resolving a conflict or approving a change set is not a review. Fragments and the Conflict Log are never verified or moved to `stable`.
12. **Index and validate** after any change:
    ```bash
    uv run "<skills>/okf-standard/scripts/okf_index.py" <bundle> --dir <directory>     # once per directory of a concept touched, e.g. base/decisions/architecture
    uv run "<skills>/okf-standard/scripts/okf_index.py" <bundle> --dir fragments       # if a fragment changed
    uv run "<skills>/okf-standard/scripts/okf_lint.py" <bundle>
    ```
    Each run regenerates that directory's `index.md` and the one of every directory above it up to the bundle root, so a directory that is new is listed by its parent. Fix any ERROR before finishing.
13. **Close**: what was decided and by whom, what is still open (conflicts, and open questions by owner, the due ones first), the concepts still awaiting review, and the concepts that still break the Domain Profile.

## Audit mode

The audit lists what the bundle is missing or gets wrong, one finding per item with the concept and the evidence, and records what the person decides on each. It changes nothing before the person decides.

1. **Find the bundle** as in step 1: its version, its OKF profile, the Domain Profile in force and its aliases.
2. **Git facts.** The reference point is only the one the person names: a commit, a tag or a date (for a date, the last commit before it); step 2's default does not apply here. Run:
   ```bash
   uv run "<skills>/okf-standard/scripts/okf_audit.py" <bundle> --since <ref> --json   # without a reference point: no --since
   ```
   It runs only read-only git (`log`, `diff`, `rev-parse`, `show`, `ls-files`, `hash-object` without `-w`), and its git facts leave out what lint leaves out (`.okfignore`, nested bundles, non-OKF directories, files that are not concepts). It also looks inside each instance the bundle holds, at any depth, with or without a reference point and inside or outside git: each `EDITED_AFTER_CLOSE` is a finding, a concept of a closed instance whose `generated.at` is later than its instance's `closed_at` (a closed instance is frozen, standard §2.2), with the `instance` it belongs to. Without a reference point, or for a bundle outside a git repository, it skips the git facts and says so in `notices`: tell the person, and that naming a commit, tag or date adds them. With one, each of its `findings` is a finding: `DELETED_UNRECORDED` (a concept or fragment deleted, neither `deprecated` at the reference commit nor recorded by an entry added since to the root `log.md` or its directory's, a **Deprecation** or one saying it was deprecated, removed or superseded; the message names untracked files of the same name) or `RENAMED_UNRECORDED` (renamed, by git's rename detection or as an untracked file of identical content, with `to`, and no entry added since naming both paths). Audit entries never record a change. Its other `notices` (an accepted-as-is item it cannot read, raised again) go to the person. `reference.commit` is the reference point's full commit.
   **Earlier audits.** Its `accepted_as_is` lists the findings earlier audits at the same reference point accepted as is (matched by the full commit, so a date and a tag of the same commit are one reference point; outside git, by the reference as typed; with none, `none`): leave each out of this audit and say how many were left out.
3. **Mechanical part**: `uv run "<skills>/okf-standard/scripts/okf_lint.py" <bundle> --json`. Group its findings and `metrics` by what a person must do, as lint reports them; never re-derive them by hand:

   | Action | Codes and metrics |
   |---|---|
   | Rotate a secret | `SECRET_IN_BUNDLE`: remove it, rotate it, scan the history with gitleaks; never show the value |
   | Restore a fragment's material | `FRAGMENT_MATERIAL_CHANGED`: put back the file its `sha256` names, or deprecate the fragment and capture a new one; never change the `sha256` to match |
   | Verify again | `VERIFIED_STALE`, `STALE`: a recorded verification (step 11), a new `stale_after` or a deprecation |
   | Distill | `FRAGMENT_NO_LEDGER`, `LEDGER_GAP`, `FRAGMENT_UNCITED`; `metrics.fragments.by_status` pending and `oldest_pending_days` (`okf-distill`) |
   | Cite | `FOOTNOTE_NO_SOURCE` |
   | Fix a link | `BROKEN_LINK`, `LINK_OUTSIDE` |
   | Fill a section | `SECTION_EMPTY` |
   | Answer or drop a question | `QUESTION_REVIEW_DUE`, `QUESTION_NO_ANSWER` |
   | Other lint findings | every other code, with lint's message |

   `metrics.concepts_by_trust` and `stale_after_cluster` are shown as figures, never as findings.
4. **Judgment part.** Read the concepts and propose, one finding per item, each with the concept's bundle path and the evidence (the lines, the links, the other concepts):
   - **Unwritten terms and systems**: a term or a system that other concepts mention and no `Term` or `System` concept (aliases and local types resolved) defines.
   - **Missing sections**: a decision (any type that is a `Decision`) without its alternatives or its consequences, a Runbook without steps or a rollback: the section absent, or present with content that says nothing lint can tell (`TBD`, `n/a`). A section lint reports as `SECTION_EMPTY` is already in the mechanical part: not raised again.
   - **Unfootnoted claims**: in a concept with `sources`, a claim of the body that no `[^id]` footnote supports.
   - **Uncited sources** (`SOURCE_UNCITED`): a `sources` entry that no `[^id]` footnote of the body cites (the reverse, a footnote with no source, is lint's `FOOTNOTE_NO_SOURCE`).
   - **Contradictions**: two concepts that state incompatible things and have no row in the Conflict Log.
   - **Links to retired targets** (`LINK_TO_RETIRED`): a concept linking to a `deprecated` concept outside that concept's own `supersedes`/`superseded_by` chain; a concept linking to an Open Question that is `answered` or `dropped`; an `answered` or `dropped` question that still lists `blocks`. Links from `index.md` and `log.md` are not findings.
   - **Free-text records**: a distilled fragment whose `# Record` has no `##` headings (bold-lead paragraphs) and a part that no row of its `# Distillation` ledger accounts for.

   A finding inside a decision (any type that is a `Decision`), whatever its `decision_status`, is offered only as accept as is, a new decision of the same type that supersedes it (`okf-capture`) or the tracker: a decision's body is never edited. The one exception is a committed secret (`SECRET_IN_BUNDLE`): it is removed from the decision and rotated, since removing a credential does not change what was decided, and the log entry says so, naming the decision and never the value.
5. **Present** the findings numbered, grouped as the git facts (`EDITED_AFTER_CLOSE` among them, under its own name), the mechanical part by action and the judgment part, with how many accepted earlier were left out. Ask the person to decide each one: **fix now** (applied with the rules of the skill that owns it: `okf-capture` for a concept, with a new `generated.at`; `okf-distill` for a fragment's ledger; step 11 for a verification), **open question** (an `Open Question` with its `owner`, `okf-capture`), **conflict** (a row in the Conflict Log, state `proposed`), **accept as is**, or **tracker** (listed for the person to file, never written into `base/`). For `EDITED_AFTER_CLOSE`, **fix now** means restoring the concept as it was at `closed_at` (from git), or carrying the change into the parent with `okf-capture`; never move `closed_at` and never reopen the instance. Suggest one if useful; never pick it. A finding not decided stays out of the record and is listed at the close.
6. **Record** the decisions as one **Update** entry under today's date in `knowledge/log.md`, in this form, with the person's `human:<id>` (ask for it):
   ```markdown
   * **Update**: Audit since `<the reference point as given, or none>` (reference `<reference.commit; outside git, the reference as typed; none>`): <N> findings, <M> left out as accepted earlier; fixed now: <links>; open questions: <links>; conflicts: <C-NNN>; tracker: <short names>; decided by human:<id>. Accepted as is: `/base/<dir>/<file>.md` (<short name of the finding>); `/fragments/<file>.md` (<short name>).
   ```
   `Accepted as is:` comes last, each item a bundle path in backticks and a short name in parentheses with no parentheses inside, so a later audit at the same reference point reads them; leave the part out when nothing was accepted. Add the **Creation**, **Update** and **Deprecation** entries of what the fixes changed, then index and validate as in step 12.
7. **Close**: what was decided and by whom, the findings left undecided, and the number left out as accepted earlier.
